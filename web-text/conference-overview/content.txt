<p style="text-align: center; font-weight: bold; font-size: 20px;">International Academic Exchange Center, Xiamen University Malaysia, Selangor, Malaysia, Month xx<sup>th</sup>-xx<sup>th</sup>, 2026</p>
<br>
<span><p>Following the successful previous editions of the Xiamen Symposium on Light–Matter Interactions, the 5th Xiamen Symposium on Light–Matter Interactions will bring together researchers and scholars from around the world at the International Academic Exchange Center of Xiamen University Malaysia, Selangor, Malaysia, for in-depth scientific exchange and collaboration. The symposium focuses on the latest advances in light–matter interactions, with particular emphasis on the fundamental mechanisms of light–matter interactions in micro- and nanostructures, ultrafast and multidimensional spectroscopic techniques, and multi-field approaches for tuning and controlling material properties. The materials systems covered include III–V quantum dots, two-dimensional semiconductors, perovskites, organic semiconductors, oxides, and other emerging material platforms. The symposium aims to promote academic exchange and collaboration among researchers in the field, facilitate the dissemination of recent scientific achievements, and foster discussions on future research directions. Distinguished scientists from leading universities and research institutions will be invited to deliver keynote and invited talks, providing participants with a valuable opportunity to exchange ideas, establish collaborations, and explore the frontiers of light–matter interaction research. </p><p><br/></p><p><strong>Conference time: </strong>Month xx-xx, 2026 </p><p><strong>Venue: </strong>International Academic Exchange Center, Xiamen University Malaysia, Selangor, Malaysia</p><p><strong>Conference organizer: </strong>School of Physical Science and Technology, Xiamen University </p><p><strong>Conference Chair: </strong>Prof. Zhanghai Chen, Xiamen University, China</p><p><strong>Organizing Chair: </strong>Prof. Long Zhang, Xiamen University, China</p><p></p><p><strong>Conference Secretary: </strong>Dr. Song Luo,Xiamen University, China</p><p><br/></p><p><strong>Important dates:</strong></p><p><strong>Abstract submission starts: </strong>TBD</p><p><strong>Abstract submission deadline: </strong>TBD</p><p><strong>Registration starts: </strong>TBD</p><p><strong>Payment deadline: </strong>TBD</p></span>
