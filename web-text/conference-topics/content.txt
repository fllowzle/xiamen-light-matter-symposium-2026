<h2>Conference Topics</h2><p>The symposium will focus on the latest advances and emerging breakthroughs in the broad field of light–matter interactions, with particular emphasis on extreme interactions between novel optical fields and matter, advanced approaches for light-field manipulation, and their applications in information technology, biomedicine, energy science, and related areas. Contributed and invited presentations are expected to cover a wide range of topics, including the theory, materials, fabrication, devices, characterization, and applications of light–matter interactions in nanostructured systems.</p>
<p><strong>The conference will cover the topics on:</strong></p>
<p>1. Light-Field Manipulation in Micro- and Nanostructure</p>
<p>2. Excitons and Exciton–Polaritons in Two-Dimensional Semiconductors</p>
<p>3. Ultrafast Optical Spectroscopy of Low-Dimensional Semiconductor Systems</p>
<p>4. Fundamental Physics and Optoelectronic Devices of Organic Semiconductors</p>
