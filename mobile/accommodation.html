Accommodation

The 5th XMU Symposium on Light–Matter Interactions in Nanostructures will be held at the International Academic Exchange Center of Xiamen University Malaysia, Selangor, Malaysia.

A limited number of rooms have been reserved for conference participants at the university guest house. Reservations will be arranged on a first-come, first-served basis. For the convenience of participants, detailed information on the on-campus accommodation, including room types, rates, booking procedures, and contact information, will be provided on the conference website. Participants are encouraged to make their reservations as early as possible due to limited availability.
