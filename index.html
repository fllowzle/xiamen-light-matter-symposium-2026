Conference Overview

5th Xiamen Symposium on Light–Matter Interactions

International Academic Exchange Center, Xiamen University Malaysia, Selangor, Malaysia, Month xxth-xxth, 2026


Following the successful previous editions of the Xiamen Symposium on Light–Matter Interactions, the 5th Xiamen Symposium on Light–Matter Interactions will bring together researchers and scholars from around the world at the International Academic Exchange Center of Xiamen University Malaysia, Selangor, Malaysia, for in-depth scientific exchange and collaboration. The symposium focuses on the latest advances in light–matter interactions, with particular emphasis on the fundamental mechanisms of light–matter interactions in micro- and nanostructures, ultrafast and multidimensional spectroscopic techniques, and multi-field approaches for tuning and controlling material properties. The materials systems covered include III–V quantum dots, two-dimensional semiconductors, perovskites, organic semiconductors, oxides, and other emerging material platforms. The symposium aims to promote academic exchange and collaboration among researchers in the field, facilitate the dissemination of recent scientific achievements, and foster discussions on future research directions. Distinguished scientists from leading universities and research institutions will be invited to deliver keynote and invited talks, providing participants with a valuable opportunity to exchange ideas, establish collaborations, and explore the frontiers of light–matter interaction research.

Conference Topics

The symposium will focus on the latest advances and emerging breakthroughs in the broad field of light–matter interactions, with particular emphasis on extreme interactions between novel optical fields and matter, advanced approaches for light-field manipulation, and their applications in information technology, biomedicine, energy science, and related areas. Contributed and invited presentations are expected to cover a wide range of topics, including the theory, materials, fabrication, devices, characterization, and applications of light–matter interactions in nanostructured systems.

The conference will cover the topics on:

1. Light-Field Manipulation in Micro- and Nanostructure

2. Excitons and Exciton–Polaritons in Two-Dimensional Semiconductors

3. Ultrafast Optical Spectroscopy of Low-Dimensional Semiconductor Systems

4. Fundamental Physics and Optoelectronic Devices of Organic Semiconductors
